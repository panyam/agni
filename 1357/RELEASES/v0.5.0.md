# v0.5.0

v0.5.0 opens boards straight out of Altium Designer and Cadence Allegro, with no ODB++ or IPC-2581
export first. It also adds cross-probing between a schematic and its board. A sheet opens in a view
beside the canvas, a pick on one shows on the other, and a board view turns and flips to match the
board on the bench. The release also reads ODB++X, draws KiCad schematics and boards much closer to
how kicad-cli draws them, and teaches several rules what a datasheet already says. It is a minor
release because it adds readers and rpcs. Nothing breaks, and everything in v0.4.0's notes holds.

## Added

- **An Altium Designer `.PcbDoc` reader** (PR 1391). `readers/altium` reads the compound file, with
  a reader of its own for the container format, into a netlist and a board. Designators are the ones
  drawn on the board, mounting holes and fiducials are left out as Altium's export leaves them, and
  part numbers come from each part's parameters. `TestAltiumPcbDocMatchesItsODBExport` holds the
  five foxBMS boards to their ODB++ jobs. `make exercise-altium` walks the master board.
- **A Cadence Allegro `.brd` reader, for 16.6 and 17.2** (PR 1365, PR 1394). It reads the binary
  board into a netlist and a board: parts, nets, every pin's position, tracks, vias and zones. Each
  copper layer is held to the film Allegro plotted for it (PR 1380), and the BeagleBone AI to the
  extract Allegro wrote from it. Other releases are refused by name, and an Eagle `.brd` is refused
  as Eagle. `make exercise-allegro` walks the BeagleBone AI.
- **ODB++X (ODX)** (PR 1379). An ODB++ job in one XML file reads as ODB++ does, and parasdr's two
  Allegro exports are held to each other.
- **Arc tracks stay arcs** (PR 1396) in the board geometry from every board reader, rather than
  turning into chords.
- **A sheet beside the canvas** (PR 1375). Any sheet opens in a view of its own, and a part, pin or
  net picked on one view is found and lit on the others. `DesignService.LocateSubjects` answers
  which sheets draw a subject, the board included (PR 1364).
- **The board under a click** (PR 1343, PR 1354). The board view keys its pads, copper and
  footprints, so a click picks a pin, a net or a part, and silkscreen and designators let a click
  through to the copper beneath.
- **A board view turns and flips** (PR 1378), by quarter turns and left to right or top to bottom,
  and shows the board from underneath with the back copper on top. Back-side text draws mirrored, as
  KiCad draws it.
- **A board the design does not declare** (PR 1395, PR 1398). "Use a board…" in the Sheets panel
  picks a board file or an ODB++ job from the design's mount and attaches it to the page
  (`?board=`). "Save to design.yaml" then asks the new `WorkspaceService.DeclareCompanion` for the
  descriptor that declares it. A dropped design saves it in the browser, and a served one shows the
  text to commit.
- **`make exercise-cross-probe`** (PR 1371, PR 1377, PR 1384) walks the cross-probe mission on a cut
  of Jetson in the gate, and on the whole board when asked.
- **An SD card profile** (PR 1297), with termination and ESD judged per bus, and an eMMC judged one
  bus at a time (PR 1293).
- **agni's agent skills ship as a plugin** (PR 1292), starting with `verify-lexicon`, for Claude
  Code, Cursor and Copilot.

## Changed

- **KiCad schematics draw as kicad-cli draws them.** A rotated symbol sits where its pins connect
  (PR 1345). Symbol fills, pin text and the DNP cross match KiCad (PR 1348). Labels sit where
  kicad-cli puts them, with their flags and arrows (PR 1363). Markup such as `~{RESET}` is drawn,
  sheet pins carry their arrows, and a placed pin's alternate name shows (PR 1390). Orientation is
  checked against Lepton and the EDIF spec in the same PR.
- **Board text draws at KiCad's size and in its layer's colour** (PR 1387).
- **`agni.yaml` paths resolve beside the file** (PR 1379), so a command reads the same mounts from a
  subfolder of the checkout.
- **`agni trial` counts a KiCad design** (PR 1385), which it read as 0 components. The proposer lists
  every ODB++ job in a folder, and a picked pin is marked on the sheet.
- **The gate runs as four parallel CI jobs** (PR 1381).
- **Docs and comments read in one pass** (PR 1305 to PR 1317). The guide, tutorials, architecture
  pages, protos and every package's comments were rewritten so no sentence makes the reader wait.
  PR 1317 fixes the bugs and stale comments that sweep found.
- **Dependencies.** jaala v0.1.92 and mcpkit v0.8.16 (PR 1302, PR 1320), the samples corpus at
  v0.1.11, and a group of npm updates (PR 1304).

## Fixed

- **A datasheet's integrated parts count** (PR 1296, PR 1300). A crystal whose oscillator holds its
  own load capacitance passes, and a current-limiting switch and an integrated pull-up are credited
  from the datasheet.
- **KiCad nets split at a sheet boundary join** (PR 1295).
- **A reused sheet's ports are left alone** (PR 1299) when only its parent straps them.
- **The sample fetch accepts an ODB++ corpus** (PR 1291), so `make odb-oracle` runs from a fresh
  clone.
