# The deferred-work ledger

Each line records one item that a PR deferred on purpose without opening a roadmap ticket. An
entry follows these rules.

- A PR that lists something under "Out of scope" adds it here in the same PR.
- An entry names the source PR (plain text, no backlink) and the natural pickup trigger.
- Remove the entry when the item merges or gets promoted to a roadmap ticket. Items that
  need evidence tracking, affect correctness, or have no adjacent future work get a
  ticket instead of a line here.
- **An entry belongs here only if nobody could pick it up deliberately.**
  If someone could schedule it, it is a ticket. If it can never be picked up at all because
  the question is settled, it is a decision and belongs in `DECISIONS.md`. That leaves a narrow
  middle. It holds work that becomes obvious and cheap the moment an unrelated trigger fires, and
  that is not worth anyone's attention before then, such as "rename this when you are next in this
  file", "add the fixture when a design that needs it turns up".
- **Sweep it periodically.** Normal work only ever appends to this file, so it drifts in two
  directions on its own. Entries remain after the thing they asked for is built, and a stale line
  reads as current, which is worse than a long list. One piece of work also gets written down many
  times, as several PRs each defer their own slice of it. Neither drift is visible one entry at a
  time, so both need a read-through to see. The 2026-08 sweep took 108 entries to 15. It promoted
  eighteen clusters and singletons to tickets, moved three settled questions to `DECISIONS.md`, and
  deleted what had already been built.
- **Nothing private goes in this file.** It is world-readable. If a pickup trigger cannot be stated
  without naming a customer, a private path, or a corpus location, it belongs in the private
  workspace, not here. Sanitize when writing the line, not later; deleting it afterwards does
  not remove it from history.
