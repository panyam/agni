# v0.6.13

v0.6.13 lets agni compare two real revisions of Antmicro's Jetson baseboard and read KiCad 10 schematics. It also shows what changed between revisions more clearly in the viewer, and a new walkthrough video steps through one change of each kind. It is a patch. No API changes.

## Added

- **Jetson revs 1.1.2 and 1.1.3 to compare with rev 1.1.0** (PRs 1561 and 1564, issue 1559). `make samples-revisions` fetches them from agni-samples' new `revisions` tarball, and `make -C examples/jetson-project revision` copies them in beside rev 1.1.0. `diff-revision` compares rev 1.1.0 with rev 1.1.3, Antmicro's current revision.
- **A walkthrough of what changed between two revisions** (PRs 1561 and 1564). `make demo-video JOURNEY=compare-revisions` records 92 seconds on Jetson rev 1.1.0 against rev 1.1.3. It steps through an added, a removed and a changed part, a new, a deleted and a renamed net, and a hard change, and each step says what that kind means. It plays on the README, the docsite's front page and the comparing-revisions guide.

## Changed

- **A comparison opens with the Changes list beside the drawings** (PR 1561). It used to be a tab behind Diff, so a reader saw the drawings or the list, never both. Closing the comparison tabs Changes back.
- **A net clicked in Changes goes to a sheet that draws it** (PRs 1563 and 1564, issue 1560). A net that crosses sheets used to stay on the top sheet, where only its sheet pins show. It now goes to the first sheet below the top that draws it, on the side the view reveals.

## Fixed

- **KiCad 10 bus aliases** (PR 1563, issue 1560). KiCad 10 keeps them in the `.kicad_pro`, and the reader read only the schematics, so no group bus crossed a sheet boundary. On Jetson rev 1.1.3, a diff from rev 1.1.2 listed 267 new nets beside 23 new parts. It now lists 14.
- **KiCad 10 hidden fields** (PR 1564). KiCad 10 writes a field's `(hide yes)` in the property itself, and every footprint name and datasheet URL drew over the parts.
- **A click in Changes keeps the list's place** (PR 1561). It used to rebuild every row and jump back to the top.

## Build and CI

- **A change to the README, the changelog, NEXTSTEPS.md or the release notes alone skips the gate** (PR 1557).
- **agni-samples v0.1.18**, whose `revisions` tarball the gate doesn't fetch, so CI downloads the same as before.
