# v0.6.12

v0.6.12 carries the last tickets of the demo mission (issue 1442). The viewer's dock moves onto goapplib's `tsappkit-dock`, so Sheets, Checks and Review slide in as drawers instead of holding two columns, and on a laptop the schematic and the board beside it get most of the window. The README and the docsite's front page play the find-a-part walkthrough. It is a patch. No API changes, and a reader's saved dock layout starts over once on the new default.

## Changed

- **Sheets, Checks and Review open as drawers** (PR 1555, issues 1549 and 1542). Sheets slides in from the left, and Checks, Review, Rules, Lexicon and Coverage tab together on the right. Header buttons beside Panels open and close them, a click on the scrim or Escape closes one, and a tab's menu docks a panel back in the grid. Choosing a sheet closes Sheets. The grid keeps Properties, the canvas and Query.
- **New shortcuts** (PR 1555, issue 1542). ⌘/Ctrl+G shows or hides Sheets, and ⌘/Ctrl+Shift+F puts the cursor in the query box. ⌘/Ctrl+F still opens Find by name. Escape with a drawer open closes the drawer and keeps the highlight.
- **A drawer left open comes back closed** on the next page, with its tabs and size kept (PR 1556, goapplib issue 148).
- **The README and the docsite's front page play the find-a-part walkthrough** (PRs 1554, 1555 and 1556). It sits under the README's `#watch-a-walkthrough` anchor and the front page's `#demo`. The README's formats table adds OrCAD Capture, Cadence Allegro, Altium, ODB++ and gEDA, and the old showcase GIF goes.

## Fixed

- **The dock keeps its sizes across visits** (PRs 1555 and 1556). A design folder's first visit, and every visit after one, opened with a half-width Properties rail and a half-height query strip, since the dock was saved before dockview had laid the grid out.
- **⌘F, ⌘⇧F and Trace from Properties focus their field** as the panel comes forward (PR 1555). The first keys a reader typed could land on the tab instead.
- **A query link no longer scrolls the whole viewer out of the window** (PR 1555). The page's shell now clips its overflow, so a focus inside a panel can't scroll it.
- **The board picker opens above the Sheets drawer**, so Use can be clicked (PR 1555).
- **Changes opens as a 260px rail right of Diff** when a comparison adds it back, not half the centre (PR 1555, issue 759).

## Build and CI

- **goapplib, `@panyam/tsappkit`, `tsappkit-solid` and `tsappkit-dock` go to 0.7.12, and dockview-core goes from 1.17 to 7.0.4** (PRs 1555 and 1556).
- **The browser suite reaches drawer panels through `showPanel` and `hideDrawers`** in `web/browser/panels.ts`, and `drawers.spec.ts` covers the drawers, the shortcuts and a reload (PRs 1555 and 1556).
