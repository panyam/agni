# v0.6.11

v0.6.11 carries most of the demo mission (issue 1442): the viewer fixes and additions a demo of agni on a real board needed, and the command that records one. On Jetson's board beside its schematic, clicking a subject in any panel now opens its sheet and glides to it, a lit net repaints its own copper, and a trace lights its route on the board as well as the schematic. `make demo-video JOURNEY=find-a-part` records the first journey as a 1080p mp4. It is a patch. `DesignService` gains a method and no existing field changes.

## Added

- **`make demo-video JOURNEY=<name>` records a demo journey as a 1080p mp4** (PR 1547, issue 1545). The journeys live in `web/browser/journeys.ts` as captioned steps that act as a reader would and check what the viewer did. A recording shows a title card, a caption before each step and a cursor. The gate plays every journey on the cut of Jetson, so a broken journey fails with the step it broke at. The first journey finds a part on the board.
- **The Trace panel offers only the end pins a trace can reach** (PR 1548, issue 1541). Once the start field names a pin, the end field lists the pins a trace from it routes to, each with its distance in series crossings, and a dropdown sets the radius for both. A rail is named with its pin count. `DesignService.ReachablePins` answers it in the server and in the browser engine, and `agni trace --from U1.3 --reachable` prints the same answer.

## Changed

- **A subject clicked in Query, Tables or Checks opens its sheet, selects it and zooms to it** (PR 1544, issue 1539). A Find by name row now goes to the sheet that draws the part, Tables locates pin findings and verdict rows, and a board view beside the canvas follows. A bound value's box in the Query panel keeps its focus while the reader types, and the answer keeps about half the panel (issue 1540).
- **A net lit on the board repaints its own copper** (PR 1550, issue 1543), in WebGL and in SVG. Its tracks keep their width, its pads and vias fill, and its pours keep their wash. The opacity is `min(1, alpha / 0.4)`, so the default reads opaque. A schematic net keeps its translucent marker.
- **A revealed subject glides into view** (PR 1552, issue 1546). A locate, a walk's start or a following sheet view moves over 300 ms instead of jumping, with the zoom on a log scale. A wheel, a drag or a new reveal takes over. A reader whose system asks for reduced motion gets the jump, and so does the browser suite.
- **A trace lights its route on the sheet views beside the canvas** (PR 1553, issue 1551). A view set to follow glides to frame the route. The presenter's `walkLit` view hook is now `underlay`, since walks and traces both use it.
- **`agni trace` takes `--reachable` in place of `--to`** (PR 1548). Without either it still refuses, with the same message.

## Build and CI

- **The old per-PR image branches are folded into `pr-assets-archive`** and deleted. A PR now commits its screenshots on its own branch and removes them before merging (`CONTRIBUTING.md`).
