# v0.6.14

v0.6.14 stops a walk from painting the nets it stopped on. On a board, a ground or a rail filled the copper in grey. It also puts the compare-revisions walkthrough first on the docsite's front page. It is a patch. No API changes.

## Changed

- **A walk lights no net it stopped on** (PR 1567, issue 1562). A ground, a rail, a global or a fan-out the walk stopped on used to be painted in faint grey. On a board, its overlapping segments stacked toward solid grey over the copper, in WebGL and in SVG. It's no longer lit in any view. The Trace tab still lists each one and says why the walk stopped there, and the hops, the parts crossed, the start and a goal's routes stay lit. Issue 1566 holds shading it again on schematics only, should a reader miss it there.
- **The docsite's front page plays the compare-revisions walkthrough first** (PR 1565), with find-a-part after it. Each keeps its anchor, `#demo-compare` and `#demo`.

## Build and CI

- **The tutorial captures' input stamps are refreshed** for the Jetson project's rev 1.1.3 fixture. The captured output is unchanged.
