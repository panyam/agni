# v0.6.0

v0.6.0 reads OrCAD Capture designs. A `.DSN` reads with every hierarchical block it keeps in a file
of its own, flattened, each part under the designator its board carries, and its pages draw as
sheets. So a Capture schematic opens beside its Allegro board with no export step. The release also
shows and hides each board layer, keeps each copper layer's pad shape, reads a board's filled copper
as copper under its net, and walks outwards from a part hop by hop. It is a minor release because
it adds a reader, rpcs and geometry fields. Nothing breaks, and everything in v0.5.0's notes holds.

## Added

- **An OrCAD Capture `.DSN` reader** (PR 1405). `readers/orcad` reads the compound file and every
  block `.DSN` it names, by the path relative to itself that each block records, matched without
  case. Parts take their designators from the root's occurrence tree, since a block file's own
  placements still say `R1` or `C?`. A pin joins a net through the wire its stored position sits on,
  a pin at the same point, its own stored net id, or its part's `PACK_SHORT`. A power symbol, port
  or off-page connector joins the wire ends inside its stored box. Clay R6 is held to `pstxnet.dat`,
  the netlist Capture handed Allegro, and every one of its 564 netted pins agrees, and to its board
  by designator. A legacy design is refused with its version named, and a SPECCTRA `.dsn` as
  SPECCTRA. `examples/orcad-design` walks it.
- **Capture pages draw** (PR 1412). Each page is a sheet, each block's page under the page that
  places it, with every symbol, field, pin name and number, wire, alias, junction, block and title
  block. Power symbols and ports name the net they touch (`SymbolPlacement.net_anchor`), so a click
  on one selects its net. The file checks its own transform: every page stores each placed pin's
  position, and all 573 of Clay's pins come out there through `geomath.PlacePin`.
- **Each board layer shows and hides** (PR 1402, PR 1403). The Board entry in the Sheets panel lists
  the board's layers in stackup order, with a checkbox and a colour swatch each. A double-click shows
  one alone, and presets show all, none, the front or the back. `GetSheetResponse.board_layers`
  carries the rows, and a hidden layer takes no clicks.
- **A padstack table** (PR 1408). `BoardGeometry.padstacks` gives each copper layer a pad's own land,
  and a `Pad` or `Via` names its entry. Allegro reads each layer's pad, thermal relief and antipad,
  and IPC-2581 and ODB++ gather each pin's lands across the layers. Both renderers draw each land in
  its layer, and an antipad as a dashed outline on a plane that does not carry the pad's net.
- **Filled copper is copper** (PR 1411, PR 1414). A plane or pour is a `CopperShape`, an outline and
  its holes, under its net in `NetCopper.shapes`. Allegro, IPC-2581, ODB++ and Altium read theirs,
  and `Zone` keeps one meaning, a region a designer drew to be filled. The SVG and WebGL renderers
  fill it, and a Pours row hides it. `board.copper(net, layer, kind)` and `board.zone(net, layer)`
  query them.
- **A walk outwards from a part** (PR 1409). `DesignService.WalkDesign` walks from a part or a pin,
  hop by hop, through the parts a walk kind crosses (`series`, `small-parts`, `all`), and says where
  and why it stopped. `agni walk <design> --from R1` prints it, the MCP server gains a `walk` tool,
  and `ListWalkKinds` lists the kinds.
- **A note when a design has no drawing** (PR 1404). `GetDesignResponse.drawing_note` says the
  sheets are agni's layout and names the file that would draw them, and a strip above the canvas
  shows it.
- **`UNEXPANDED_REASON_FILE_MISSING`** (PR 1405). A Capture block whose file is missing is listed
  with the file it records, and the hierarchy note says so.

## Changed

- **The canvas bar has no layout picker** (PR 1404). A design draws as its schematic does, or on
  agni's default layout. `?layout=`, `?symbols=` and the CLI's `--layout` are unchanged.
- **IPC-2581 and ODB++ fills read as copper shapes** (PR 1411), with their holes, rather than as
  zones. A consumer that read those fills from `BoardGeometry.zones` reads them from
  `NetCopper.shapes` now.
- **A KiCad zone on several layers reads once per layer** (PR 1411).
- **The compound-file reader moved** from `readers/altium` to `internal/cfb` (PR 1405), so Altium
  and Capture share it.
- **The gate fails on a tracked binary** or a file over 5 MB (`make binaries-check`, PR 1409).

## Fixed

- **Board layers keep the stackup's order** on a KiCad 9 board, whose layer numbers do not
  (PR 1403), and a double-click on a hidden layer shows it alone instead of hiding every layer.
- **The Allegro film check** fills Gerber regions and their clear-polarity voids (PR 1411).
- **The `orcad-design` example's binary** is no longer tracked (PR 1409, PR 1412).
