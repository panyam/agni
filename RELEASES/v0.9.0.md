# v0.9.0

v0.9.0 carries mission 1691, so a server keeps designs. A person uploads a board, or adds one from a mounted folder, and the server keeps it as a record with its files by hash. Each design holds artifacts, keeps reports checked against packs of rules, and says when its files or the rules changed since a report. The viewer has pages for designs, projects, packs and reports. It also carries most of mission 1722, linked sources. A runtime mount snapshots a GitHub repo, private ones through a connection, and an artifact links to a path in a mount at a ref and follows a branch. Mission 1625 finishes, since `agni serve` and `agnids` ask every caller for a token, serve TLS, and every client trusts a team's own CA. Mission 1664 finishes too, since each part on a board draws as its own 3D model. It is a minor release with several breaking changes, each under Breaking with its migration.

## Breaking

- **`DesignService` is now `DesignViewService`, and its `GetDesign` is `GetSummary`** (PR 1707, issue 1705). `design.proto` is `design_view.proto`, and the Go types are `service.DesignViewService` and `server.DesignView`. Migration: rename the calls. TypeScript's accessor is `client.designView`, Java's `designViews()`, Rust's `design_views()`, and Python's `get_summary`. The name `DesignService` now serves stored designs.
- **A later version of a board is a design of its own** (PR 1703, issue 1692). `Design.revisions` and `DesignRevision` are removed, and `Design.series` arrives. A `design.yaml` that still lists `revisions:` fails at that line with a hint. Migration: move each revision into its own folder with its own `design.yaml`, and give the designs one `series`.
- **A project is a list of designs** (PR 1717, issue 1694). `ProjectService.GetDesign` and `ListDesigns` are removed. Migration: read `Project.designs`, then call `DesignService.GetDesign` or `ListDesigns` with `project = "projects/..."`.
- **`--store` and `--uploads` replace `--state-dir` and `--review-store`** (PRs 1741 and 1702). `--store file:///...` keeps records, meaning designs, projects, packs, reports, snapshot definitions, review runs, mounts, connections and tokens. `--uploads file:///...` keeps each uploaded file once, by hash. There are no aliases. Migration: pass `--store` where you passed `--state-dir` or `--review-store`, and add `--uploads` to keep designs.
- **A folder in a mount makes no design** (PR 1755, issue 1736). A server's designs are its store's records alone, and a server with mounts and no `--store` lists none. Migration: add a folder's boards with `agni designs add <mount>/<path> --remote ...`, or from the folder's panel under `/mounts/`.
- **The compose bundle keeps everything on one volume, `data`, at `/var/lib/agni`** (PRs 1731 and 1741). The records go in `meta/` and the uploaded files in `files/`. Migration: copy the old `reviews` volume's contents into `meta/reviews/` on the new volume before upgrading.
- **Go embedding API** (PR 1762, issue 1759). The `artifact` package's `URI` is removed, and its callers use goapplib storage's `mounts.URI`. The `mounts` package is now `mountset`, and `mountset.Folder` replaces `mounts.Mount{Name, Root}`. `service.MountMaker.CreateMount` takes a connection id (PR 1764). Migration: change the imports and types. The behaviour is the same apart from goapplib's stricter path rules under Changed.
- **`DatasheetService` loses `ListMounts` and `ListDir`** (PR 1744, issue 1734). agnids serves the engine's own `WorkspaceService`. Migration: call `WorkspaceService.ListMounts` and `ListDir` on agnids.

## Added

### Keeping designs on a server

- **Stored designs, `designs/{design}`** (PRs 1702 and 1717, issues 1693 and 1694). `DesignService` creates, reads, lists, replaces and deletes them. `ListDesigns` filters by series, project or source, and marks each series' latest.
- **Artifacts** (PR 1718, issue 1696). A design holds `designs/{d}/artifacts/{a}`, each one source of the design, such as a schematic with its sheets, a netlist, a board or a BOM. `UpdateArtifact` replaces one in a write, and the server writes the design's `design.yaml` itself. `agni designs create|list|get|artifacts|put|rm|push` and `agni projects create|add` drive them.
- **Ask about a stored design by name** (PR 1720, issue 1695). `Snapshot.design = "designs/{d}"` names it, and `agni check`, `diff` and `query` take `designs/<id>` under `--remote` and send nothing.
- **Upload from the viewer** (PR 1721, issue 1719). `/designs/` lists the server's designs by series, makes one from a dropped folder, and replaces or deletes an artifact. `ImportDesignFiles` groups a folder's files into artifacts, as `agni designs push` does.
- **Packs of rules** (PR 1745). A pack, `packs/{pack}`, keeps what a `project.yaml` holds. `Snapshot.packs` layers packs into a read, and `agni check designs/<d> --pack <k> --remote` uses it. A stored design keeps its own intent.
- **Reports** (PRs 1747 and 1748, issue 1746). `CreateReport` keeps a check of a stored design against packs as `designs/{d}/reports/{r}`, with the design's and each pack's hash. `GetReport` and `ListReports` say when either changed since. A stored project names default packs. `agni review` and `check --verdicts` take `--pack`.
- **Pages for designs, projects, packs and reports** (PR 1751, issue 1697). A design's page shows its reports as tabs, and "Validate against..." keeps a new one. `/projects/` and `/packs/` make and edit them.
- **The viewer on a stored design under packs** (PR 1752, issues 1697 and 1753). `/designs/<id>/view` opens the viewer with `design=` and `pack=`, so its checks give the report's findings. Each finding in a report tab opens the viewer on that finding. `ListReports` leaves each run out and gives `finding_count`, so two tabs download kilobytes rather than 17.6 MB.
- **Add a mounted folder's boards as designs** (PRs 1755 and 1757, issue 1736). `AddDesigns` proposes one design per board and keeps the ones named, copying their files by hash. A folder's panel under `/mounts/` ticks and adds them.
- **Symbol folders travel with a design** (PR 1762). An added design copies the symbol folders its `design.yaml` names as an `ARTIFACT_TYPE_SYMBOLS` artifact, so a gEDA, Lepton or xschem design reads its parts' pins alone. A project's own symbol folders are shared through its pack, and `--no-symbol-copy` leaves a design's own out.

### Linked sources

- **Mount a GitHub repo while the server runs** (PR 1750, issue 1735). `WorkspaceService.CreateMount` snapshots `github://<owner>/<repo>` at a ref into `--uploads`, serves it read-only at once, and keeps it across a restart. agnids lists the same runtime mounts.
- **`agni mounts ls|add|rm`, and a form on `/mounts/`** (PR 1754, issue 1749).
- **Link an artifact to a mount at a ref** (PR 1758, issue 1723). `LinkArtifact` reads an artifact's files from a mount and keeps them by hash. `Artifact.source` names the mount, path, ref and commit. `SyncDesign` moves followed links to the newest commit, downloading only the files that changed. `agni designs link`, `unlink` and `sync`.
- **Private repos through connections** (PR 1764, issue 1724). `ConnectionService` keeps a GitHub App installation or a token, `connections/{c}`, each naming where its secret is, `env:NAME` or `file:/abs/path`, never the secret. `agni mounts add --connection <id>` reads a private repo through one, and `agni connections add|ls|rm` drives them.
- **Mounts at `/mounts/`** (PR 1744, issue 1734). `/files/` and older `/designs/<mount>/...` links redirect there. `WorkspaceService.GetMount` returns one mount.

### Serving a team

- **Tokens on every call** (PR 1766, issue 1627). `agni serve --auth tokens` refuses a call without a good token as `unauthenticated`, in Connect, gRPC or gRPC-Web. `agni token create|ls|revoke` keeps the tokens hashed in `--store`. A browser signs in at `/signin`. A review run records who asked in `results.meta.created_by`, and `GetServerInfo` lists `auth`.
- **TLS** (PR 1766). `--tls-cert` and `--tls-key` serve HTTPS, with HTTP/2 for gRPC clients. `--auth` without TLS starts only on localhost.
- **agnids checks the same tokens** (PR 1766, issue 1765), from the `--store` it shares with `agni serve`. `agni serve` sends `AGNI_TOKEN` on its `--params-url` calls, and the CLI's `--remote` sends it too.
- **Every client trusts a team's own CA** (PR 1766, issue 1765). C++ takes `ca_file` and now speaks `https://`, Rust has `connect_with_ca`, Java's `create` takes `caFile`, and TypeScript on Node takes `ca`. `make exercise-clients` runs all five examples over TLS with a token.

### The board in 3D

- **Each part draws as its own 3D model** (PRs 1690 and 1700, issue 1652). `GetBoardMeshes` serves the meshes a page at a time, fitted to a triangle budget, and the stack view's Board look draws them. A Quality control picks the budget.
- **Model path variables, and glTF models** (PR 1689, issue 1687). `--model-var NAME=dir` maps a variable such as Antmicro's `${ANT3DMDL}`, and a named `.step` also tries a `.gltf` or `.glb` beside it. Jetson's parts stand at their models' heights.

## Changed

- **Mounts run on goapplib storage** (PR 1762, issues 1759 and 1742). An older store's mount records are rewritten once at startup. goapplib's rules are stricter. A link in a `--mount` folder is followed only to a target inside the mount by a relative path, and a tarball path that climbs out of the repo fails the snapshot. The uploads sweep runs hourly as well as at startup.
- **agnids keeps a datasheet's doc-IR and annotations in its corpus store** (PR 1744), under `derived/<sha256 of the PDF>/`, so a mount can be read-only.
- **Every third-party download is checked against a hash** (PR 1740, issues 1737 and 1738). Actions name a commit, images a digest, and pip installs from hashed locks (C37).
- **Dependencies.** goapplib storage v0.7.15, oneauth v0.3.8 with its `grpc` module, servicekit v0.2.3 and goutils v0.1.15 (PRs 1760, 1762 and 1766).

## Fixed

- **An ODB++ record ending in `;ID=<n>` reads** (PR 1688, issue 1685). A `.comp_height` that doesn't parse draws at the typical height and is named in `BoardGeometry.read_notes`. The Lexicon panel keeps a rename the next edit overtakes (issue 1683).
- **An IPC-2581 pin no copper land names reads as a hole or no pad** (PR 1706, issue 1679), as KiCad draws it.
- **The walks wait for the models' answer on every board** (PR 1701).
- **CI fetches the samples once per shard, in parallel** (PR 1763), and pins agni-samples v0.1.21 with KiCad's and Antmicro's models (PR 1684).
