# v0.6.6

v0.6.6 carries one PR. A board draws every layer in KiCad's colour. The release is a patch, though
it renames fields on `render.Style`, as the Breaking section says.

## Changed

- **Every board layer draws in KiCad's colour** (PR 1476). Each side's silkscreen, fab and
  courtyard layer has a colour of its own, so a back-side designator reads apart from a front-side
  one. Copper takes KiCad's colours too. KiCad colours an inner layer by its number, so `InN.Cu`
  takes KiCad's colour for N, and In5 is lavender on any board. An inner layer named otherwise, as
  an Allegro or Altium board may have, takes the colour for its place among the inner layers. The
  colours were read off kicad-cli's export, In1 to In30 included. The dark theme uses them
  unchanged. The light theme darkens the few under 1.5:1 on its page to 2:1 in the same hue, since
  KiCad's pale front silk all but vanishes there. Both renderers and the Layers list's swatches
  take the same colours.

## Breaking

- `render.Style` replaces `Silk` and `Fab` with `SilkFront`, `SilkBack`, `FabFront`, `FabBack`,
  `CourtyardFront` and `CourtyardBack`, and `CopperInners` grows from 6 entries to 30. Migrate by
  setting both sides where you set `Silk` or `Fab`, and by listing up to 30 inner colours, entry
  N for `InN.Cu`. Nothing outside `core/render` set them.
