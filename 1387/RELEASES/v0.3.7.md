# v0.3.7

v0.3.7 gives every sheet of a hierarchical EDIF schematic its own id and name, and makes the viewer's
Sheets dock nest sheets under their parents and explain its counts. Everything in v0.3.6's notes
holds, including its descriptor migration.

## Changed

- **The Sheets dock nests and names sheets the way the design does** (PR 1145). Each sheet sits under
  its parent, and a parent folds, as in the file tree. A sheet name two sheets share shows the
  sheet's path instead (`Power/1`, `IO/1`), with the full id on hover. A flat design stays a flat list.
- **The dock's count pills explain themselves** (PR 1145). The red pill's hover splits its number,
  as in `5 defects on this sheet, 2 drawn here alone and 3 also on other sheets`, and a legend under
  the list explains both pills once a run has counted. A finding still counts on every sheet its
  subject is drawn on, and `architecture/web-picking.md` records why.

## Fixed

- **Every page of a hierarchical EDIF export has its own id** (PR 1145). An export whose block cells
  each name their page alike, `(page (rename &1 "1"))`, read every one of them as the same sheet, so a
  finding's sheet badge, opening a sheet and the per-sheet counts could land on any of them. A page
  id that repeats now takes its owning cell view as a prefix (`&block_b/&v_b/&1`), and a repeated name
  its cell's name (`BlockB / 1`). A page whose id and name are already unique reads exactly as before,
  so a link to such a sheet still opens it.

## Added

- **A hierarchical KiCad fixture of our own** (PR 1145), `readers/kicad/testdata/hier_numeric_root.kicad_sch`,
  with `Power` and `IO` under the root and a sub-sheet named `1` under each. `make serve` mounts it as
  `kicad`.
