# v0.3.15

v0.3.15 fixes how EDIF designs locate their parts and nets on the drawn schematic. An export that
renumbers parts after layout no longer leaves those parts drawn nowhere. EDIF net findings now carry
the sheets their net is drawn on, and a schematic that fails to read says so instead of reporting
every part as undrawn. The contract gains one locate reason and one geometry field, both additive.

## Fixed

- **A back-annotated EDIF part locates on its sheet** (PR 1229, issue 1228). An export can renumber
  parts after layout, recording the new designator in the design's
  `(viewMap (instanceBackAnnotate ...))` while the page still draws the old one, or none. Both the
  netlist and the schematic reads now take the back-annotated designator for a top-level instance, so
  a part the netlist calls `R1001` and the page draws as `R4` locates on its sheet rather than
  reading `LOCATE_REASON_NO_GEOMETRY`. Its drawn label shows the new name. Instances inside a block
  keep their page designator for now.
- **EDIF net findings carry their sheets** (PR 1232, issue 1230). A net finding looked its sheets up
  by net id, and only KiCad's geometry stamps one on a wire, so every EDIF net finding was badged with
  no sheet while a query placed the same net. Geometry without wire ids now resolves a net by name.
  KiCad keeps the id lookup, so two nets sharing a name never share sheets there.

## Added

- **`LOCATE_REASON_SCHEMATIC_UNREADABLE`** (PR 1232). A schematic that fails to read used to leave
  every subject at `LOCATE_REASON_NO_GEOMETRY`, which means the drawing leaves it out. Check, query,
  review and the check report now answer the new reason, the viewer says the schematic could not be
  read, and the CLI warns once when a declared companion fails, naming the parse error. An older
  client shows its generic "could not locate" text for it.
- **`SchematicGeometry.back_annotated`** (PR 1232) lists the placements back-annotation relabelled,
  with the designator drawn on the page and the sheet. `agni render` and every command that reads the
  schematic print it on stderr, so an export's renumbering can be checked against the drawing.

