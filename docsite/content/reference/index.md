Material to look things up in rather than to read straight through. Each page stands alone, so
nothing here assumes you read the one before it.

- The **[Rules catalog](rules/)** lists every check rule the catalog ships (built-in,
  design-intent, datalog, and interface-profile), grouped by category and flagged by source, each
  with its full reference. It is generated from the shipped catalog.
- The **[Relations catalog](relations/)** lists every query relation the fact base exposes, grouped
  by kind. It is generated from the shipped fact base.
- The **[Glossary](terms/)** defines the terms the rest of the site uses without re-teaching, each
  one a hoverable popover wherever it appears.
- **[The software analogy](analogy/)** maps EE concepts to software ones, for engineers coming from
  code.
- **[Pins and packages](pins-and-packages/)** explains why a pin number belongs to the plastic body
  rather than to the chip, and what that means for joining a design to a datasheet.
- The **[EDIF netlist primer](edif-primer/)** covers the EDIF 2.0.0 netlist format Agni ingests.
- The **[EDIF schematic primer](edif-schematic-primer/)** covers the `.eds` schematic export, its
  geometry, orientation, and join keys.
