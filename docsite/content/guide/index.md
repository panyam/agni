These guides are for people who use Agni on a design. You do not need to read any Go.

Each page here documents one feature on its own, so reach for them when you already know the name
of the thing you need. If you are starting from nothing, the [tutorials](../tutorials/) take one board
from first read through to a house checklist gating CI, adding one capability at a time.

- **[Getting started](getting-started/)** takes you from install to your first check and its report.
- **[Concepts](concepts/)** maps the ideas behind the tool back to bench intuition.
- **[Checks and reports](checks-and-reports/)** runs the rule catalog and explains its findings.
- **[Comparing revisions](comparing-revisions/)** diffs two revisions as a schematic redline.
- **[Querying](querying/)** searches a design as data, with every answer cited.
- **[Naming conventions](naming-conventions/)** encodes house style and checks it on every export.
- **[Interface profiles](interface-profiles/)** declares a bus's signals and checks every design against it.
- **[Design intent](design-intent/)** declares what a board is supposed to contain, and catches it drifting.
- **[Datasheets](datasheets/)** compares a design against a part's real limits.
- **[Running the server](running-the-server/)** brings the viewer up with Docker over your mounted designs.
- **[CLI reference](cli-reference/)** lists every command and flag.
